# PDFShift for AI Agents & Automation Tools

Powerful PDF generation for your automated workflows. Convert HTML to PDF seamlessly in your favorite automation tools and AI agents.

## Why Choose PDFShift for Automation

- **No Code Required**: Use your favorite interface: N8n, Lovable, Base44, ... or via our MCP. No programming knowledge needed.
- **API First**: RESTful API that works with any automation tool or AI agent framework.
- **Always Reliable**: 99.99% uptime guarantee. Your workflows run smoothly without interruptions.
- **Async Processing**: Webhook support for large jobs. Process documents in the background efficiently.

## Popular Integrations for PDFShift

Connect PDFShift with your favorite tools and start automating PDF generation today.

- [MCP](/agents/mcp): Give AI agents access to PDFShift through the Model Context Protocol.
- [N8N](/agents/n8n): Generate PDFs automatically from your N8N workflows.
- [Lovable](/agents/lovable): Add secure, server-side PDF export to your Lovable app in one prompt.
- [Base44](/agents/base44): Connect PDFShift from its OpenAPI spec or a backend function in Base44.
- [Clay](/agents/clay): Generate personalized PDFs from your Clay workflow data.
- [Make](/agents/make): Generate PDFs automatically from your Make scenarios.
- [Bubble](/agents/bubble): Add automated PDF generation to your Bubble applications.
- [FlowMattic](/agents/flowmattic): Generate PDFs automatically from your WordPress workflows.
- [Zapier](/agents/zapier): Create Zaps that generate PDFs from your triggers and data.

Can't find your tool? [Suggest an integration](/contact).

## What You Can Build

Real-world use cases for automated PDF generation:

- **Invoice & Receipt Generation**: Turn payment or order webhooks into branded invoices and receipts, emailed to customers instantly.
- **Report Creation**: Convert spreadsheet, database, or API data into polished PDF reports on a schedule.
- **Contract Automation**: Merge CRM data into contract templates and generate signature-ready agreements.
- **On-Demand Agent Exports**: Let an AI agent produce a PDF from a chat or MCP call: summaries, briefs, or generated content.
- **Web & Email Archiving**: Snapshot web pages, emails, or dashboards as PDFs into your cloud storage automatically.
- **Certificates & Documents at Scale**: Generate personalized certificates, tickets, or letters in bulk from a single template.
