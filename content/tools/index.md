# Free tools

Check your HTML before your customer sees it as a PDF. These run in your browser, and nothing you paste is uploaded.

- [HTML viewer](/tools/html-viewer/): paste or open HTML and see it rendered as you type, at desktop, phone or A4 page width. Turn on Print CSS to see what your `@media print` rules change.

When it looks right, send the same HTML to PDFShift and get the PDF back. The free plan gives you 50 credits a month, and you don't need a credit card. [Create an account](/register/).
